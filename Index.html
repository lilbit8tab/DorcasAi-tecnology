What is DorcasAi?

DorcasAi is a planetary awareness intelligence stack. It combines time‑cycle awareness, emotional intelligence, and global context to power products that feel less like tools and more like understanding partners.

Core Layer

Fundamental Awareness Engine

Understands time, cycles, and world‑state signals to keep every product context‑aligned with reality.

Human Layer

Emotional Intelligence Engine

Reads tone, stress, and intent to respond in ways that de‑escalate, support, and guide instead of overwhelm.

Planet Layer

Planetary Context Layer

Ingests weather, geography, and environmental signals to make decisions that are grounded in the physical world.

Flagship Products

Three flagship products demonstrate how DorcasAi turns awareness into value across communication, memory, and care.

Linguistic Clarity

UnBabble AI

A class‑diglossia dissolver that removes “noise” between language groups, restoring clarity, dignity, and intent in communication.

  • Meaning‑first translation and rewriting
  • Tone harmonization for conflict‑heavy exchanges
  • Bias and class‑coded language filtering
Memory & State

MirrorZip

Visual‑state file storage and reconstruction that remembers how systems looked and felt, not just what they stored.

  • Snapshot‑based state capture
  • Fast rollback to known‑good configurations
  • Ideal for ops, compliance, and forensics
Care & Support

DorcasAi Caregiver Companion

An emotionally intelligent companion for caregivers that tracks patterns, anticipates burnout, and offers grounded, human‑sounding support.

  • Daily emotional check‑ins
  • Context‑aware suggestions and reminders
  • Designed for multi‑generation accessibility
